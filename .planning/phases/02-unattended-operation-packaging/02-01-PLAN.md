## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal (verbatim): The collector runs by itself every hour on the Moscow clock, survives
failures and restarts without manual intervention, and the whole stack starts with one
`docker compose up -d` (or without Docker for local development).

<objective>
Deliver the unattended hourly engine as two commands an operator can run: `wbmon healthcheck` (is the
scheduler alive?) and `wbmon scheduler` (start a fresh `wbmon collect` child at every :00 MSK, catch up the
current slot once within 45 minutes, never back-fill, never overlap, survive child failures, keep a heartbeat
ticking, stop cleanly on SIGTERM). Proven by a real-process end-to-end test that never spawns a collect and by
a fake-clock behaviour matrix.

Purpose: SCHED-02 (hourly trigger, catch-up, no back-fill, no overlap) and SCHED-03 (survives failures and
restarts, heartbeat signal for healthchecks and the Phase 3 UI).
Output: `src/wbmon/{health,schedule,signals,scheduler}.py`, `scheduler`/`healthcheck` subcommands in
`src/wbmon/cli.py`, five test modules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

ROADMAP Phase 2 Goal (verbatim): The collector runs by itself every hour on the Moscow clock, survives
failures and restarts without manual intervention, and the whole stack starts with one
`docker compose up -d` (or without Docker for local development).

<objective>
Close the phase goal: stopping or restarting the collector never leaves a run hanging and never costs history.
`wbmon collect` turns SIGTERM into honest `terminated` attempt rows and a closed run; the scheduler's real
SIGTERM forwarding and SIGKILL escalation are proven with real processes; a SIGKILLed run becomes `abandoned`
and its slot stays an honest gap; and the packaging from 02-02 is cross-checked against the CLI from 02-01 so
`docker compose up -d` cannot crash-loop on a wrong command. Ends with the full offline gate on 3.11/3.12/3.14.

Purpose: SCHED-03 (survives failures, kills and restarts without manual steps), SCHED-02 (no double run, no
back-fill after interruption), OPS-01 (compose commands/healthchecks consistent with the code).
Output: edits to `models.py`, `orchestrator.py`, `cli.py`, `tests/test_contracts.py`; four new test modules.
</objective>
